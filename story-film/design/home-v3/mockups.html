Home v3 · your stuff, Loop and the reel viewer

iPhone mockups for design/home-v3/spec.md, using the v3.0 Masala Pop tokens. The Hero holds the one primary action. Rails show your own reels, songs, recordings, duets, gifts and recipes, each one tap from playing. There is one player: it's a Loop pill when idle and the mini bar when playing. Reels open full screen, Instagram-style.

Cut from Home (needs sign-off, spec §0): the Ask, Song Studio and New on Listen cards. Studio and Listen are tabs, and Ask is under Studio, Lab. The rails show your own work instead.

Contrast. The pill and bar use white on deep plum (14:1) with a plum-on-mint icon (11:1). Viewer text is white on a 60% black scrim, 7:1 or better. Icon buttons sit on 45% black circles, so they stay 3:1 or better on light frames. Every target is 44 pt or more.
