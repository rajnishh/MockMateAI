# App Store listing: Reelkar (iPhone)

Paste these into App Store Connect → Reelkar → App Store → iOS App → version 1.0. Character limits are
Apple's; every field below fits them. Screenshots are in `screenshots/` (see its README).

Don't name other companies' apps (Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, Suno) in the name, subtitle or
keywords; Apple rejects that under guideline 2.3.7. The description may say you can *post to*
Instagram and YouTube, because that's a feature.
