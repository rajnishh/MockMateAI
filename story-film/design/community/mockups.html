Decisions in these mocks. The gift page’s only primary is Reply with your own song; opening the gift is a deep button on the saffron hero (chilli on saffron fails 3:1, same rule as the Hum it mic). The tap that opens the gift starts the audio, so there is no autoplay trick. The sender sees opened, play count and last played; never who, where or which device. The note is kept out of the WhatsApp preview to keep the surprise.

Voice rule. A song in someone’s own cloned voice plays for everyone, but the reply and each Jugalbandi part can only be sung by an AI singer or the person’s own consented voice.
