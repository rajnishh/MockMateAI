Reel recipes that earn credits

Reelkar community feature 3, on Masala Pop v3.0. A maker shares a finished reel as a recipe; anyone makes their own version with their topic and photo; the maker earns 10% of the paid credits spent. Tap inside a phone to move through the flow, or pick a screen from the index. Spec: design/community/recipes-spec.md. The 10% share is a placeholder; prices are the catalog's.

What a recipe keeps, and what you bring

Everything a recipe keeps is a field the existing pipelines already accept, so a recipe replays exactly. It's a snapshot made when the recipe is shared.

Story with pictures

Keeps

Look, length, script shape (hook, beats, payoff), camera moves, the music track, captions

You bring

Your topic, your photo (optional, AI animation or Cinematic), your language, quality

Motion reel

Keeps

Theme colour, slide plan (title → stat → list → cta), ending, length, music

You bring

Your topic, your language, your brand look and comment word

Kids dance

Keeps

Scene, beat, cartoon dancers' looks, moves per line, voice (sung, chant or AI song)

You bring

Your topic, your language, dancer names (optional)

Edit my video

Keeps

Captions, zooms, effects mix, sounds, voice polish, ending

You bring

Your own video (up to 60 s), the language you speak

Never in a recipe: the maker's idea and words, title, characters, photo, face, any cloned voice, uploaded video, brand kit, comment word or lyrics. A preview that shows real people stays off until the maker confirms they agreed.

Decisions in these mocks. A recipe is a snapshot of things the pipelines can replay. On Create, the recipe locks format, look, length, music and script shape; the user's topic, photo, language and quality stay theirs. "Make it without the recipe" is always one tap away, and then the maker earns nothing.

Earnings, honestly. One model shared with remix chains (ledger reason earning, one creator_earning table, one Earnings screen). 10% of the paid part of what's spent, only on finished reels, never taken back. The paid part is in proportion to how many of the user's credits were bought. Your own uses never earn. Numbers shown are rounded down, so they never overstate.

Legally required only. Every public recipe has Report; pages, tiles and posts carry "Made with AI". No other gates.
